Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use the Service, we also store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so that we can display and summarize them for you.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible through your authenticated account. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the Service to you. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (for example, hosting or font delivery services) to operate the Service. These providers process data only to the extent necessary to deliver their service and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
